Scope and ownership

All Angular UI, layouts, feature components, shared helpers, tests and assembly scripts live under maretha-ai-web/angular, organized into features, layouts and shared. The core remains generic.

Included: CP settings; document chat and interactions; saved responses; metadata update/enhancement; embedding search; similar documents; saved searches; and all 17 source layout resources. Group gates retain AI-CHAT, AI-INTERACTION, AI-METADATA and AI-SEARCH, plus administrator access.

Not available in this Angular port / excluded: independent 3D viewer, authoring, template-rendering, workflow, portal and work-queue add-on functionality. Their installed baseline packages were left unchanged; they were not ported or exhaustively retested. The original Polymer UI reloaded and remained healthy.
